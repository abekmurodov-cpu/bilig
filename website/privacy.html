Bilig

Privacy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Bilig is a study app. It is built so that your notes belong to you: there is no account, no sign-up and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your notes, photos, practice history and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run a server that receives them, and we cannot see them. Backups you save are files you choose where to keep.

What we collect

Nothing. Bilig has no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting that sends data to us.

Optional AI features

If you connect an AI service in Settings (for example Google Gemini, Groq, OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Mistral, xAI or OpenRouter) with your own key, the text, photos or voice recordings you ask the AI to work on are sent directly from your device to that service, and are handled under that service's own privacy policy. Your key is kept in your device's secure storage. Nothing is sent to an AI service unless you set one up.

Optional Telegram backup

If you turn on Telegram sync, Bilig sends your backup to the Telegram chat you choose, through the bot you set up. This only happens if you turn it on.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos — only when you add a photo to a note or turn a page into notes.
  • Microphone and speech recognition — only while you dictate a note.
  • Notifications — to remind you when notes are due for review.

Children

Bilig does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete notes in the app, or uninstall Bilig to remove everything it stored on your device.
